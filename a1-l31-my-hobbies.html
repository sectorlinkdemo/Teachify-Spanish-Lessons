My Hobbies

Free Time & Leisure

Section 1
Vocabulary
hobby
an activity you enjoy in your free time
"My hobby is reading books."
free time
time when you are not working or studying
"In my free time, I like to cook."
enjoy
to get pleasure from something
"I enjoy playing tennis at the weekend."
music
sounds arranged in a pleasant or interesting way
"I listen to music every morning."
painting
making a picture using paint and a brush
"She loves painting landscapes."
cooking
preparing food by heating it
"Cooking is a very useful hobby."
reading
looking at and understanding written words
"Reading helps you learn new words."
gardening
growing and looking after plants and flowers
"He spends the weekend gardening."
cycling

"Cycling is popular in the Netherlands."

Section 2
Grammar Focus

Like + -ing / Love + -ing / Enjoy + -ing. I like reading. She loves cooking. He enjoys cycling. Question: What do you like doing in your free time? Negative: I don't like gardening.

Section 3
Reading

People have many different hobbies. Some people enjoy active hobbies like cycling, swimming, or hiking. Other people prefer quiet hobbies like reading, painting, or gardening.

Maria works in an office from Monday to Friday. In her free time, she loves cooking. She tries new recipes from different countries every weekend. Her family and friends love eating her food!

Pablo is very active. He enjoys cycling in the mountains near his city. He also likes photography. He takes photos of nature on his bike rides.

Hobbies are important. They help you relax after work. They also help you meet new people and learn new skills. What hobbies do you have?
