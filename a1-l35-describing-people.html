Describing People

People & Appearances
⏱ 60 min 📚 10 vocab words 🎯 People & Appearances
Section 1
Vocabulary
tall
having a greater than average height
"He is very tall — over six feet."
short
having less than average height
"She is quite short but very energetic."
thin
having little body fat; slim
"He was thin when he was young."
curly
hair that forms curves or spirals
"She has beautiful curly dark hair."
straight
hair that goes in one direction without curving
"He has straight brown hair."
beard
hair that grows on a man's face
"My manager has a short beard."
glasses
a frame with lenses worn in front of the eyes
"She always wears glasses at work."
friendly
kind and pleasant to other people
"He is very friendly and easy to talk to."
confident
feeling sure about yourself and your abilities
"She seems very confident in meetings."
appearance
the way someone looks
"Professional appearance is important at work."

Section 2
Grammar Focus

Describing with be + adjective: He is tall. She is friendly. Describing with have: She has curly hair. He has a beard. Combining: She is tall and slim with long straight hair. He is medium height with glasses and a short beard.

Section 3
Reading

Meet the Teachify team!

Carla is the head of sales. She is tall and slim with short dark hair. She always wears smart clothes and looks very professional. She is confident and friendly — perfect for sales!

Miguel is the technology manager. He is medium height with curly hair and glasses. He is quiet but very intelligent. He always has new ideas for improving the platform.

Laura is the customer success manager. She has long blonde hair and a big smile. She is very organised and always helpful. Clients love working with her.

All three work well together. Different appearances, different personalities — but the same goal.
