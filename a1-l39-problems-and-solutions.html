Problems and Solutions

Work Challenges
⏱ 60 min 📚 10 vocab words 🎯 Work Challenges
Section 1
Vocabulary
problem
a difficult situation that needs a solution
"We have a problem with the delivery schedule."
solution
an answer or way to fix a problem
"I think I have a solution to this issue."
issue
a problem or difficulty (more formal)
"There is an issue with the invoice."
mistake
something done incorrectly
"I made a mistake in the report. I am sorry."
fix
to repair or correct something
"Can you fix this error before the meeting?"
urgent
needing immediate attention
"This is urgent — the client needs an answer today."
complaint
when someone says they are not happy with something
"We received a complaint from the client."
apologise
to say sorry for something you did wrong
"I need to apologise for the delay."
help
to do something useful for another person
"Can you help me with this problem?"
understand
to know the meaning of something
"I do not understand this part of the contract."

Section 2
Grammar Focus

Reporting problems: There is a problem with... / I have an issue with... Apologising: I am sorry for... / I apologise for the delay. Asking for help: Can you help me? / Could you check this? Offering solutions: I think we should... / Why don't we...?

Section 3
Reading

It is Monday morning at Techco. Carlos, the project manager, has a problem.

A client called Ana to say there is a mistake in the invoice. The total is wrong — it shows 5,000 euros but it should be 4,200 euros.

Carlos speaks to the accounts team. 'We need to fix this today,' he says. 'It is urgent.'

The accounts manager, Paula, checks the invoice. 'You are right. I am sorry. I made a mistake. I will send a correct invoice immediately.'

Carlos calls the client. 'I am calling to apologise for the error in your invoice. We are sending a corrected version right now.'

The client is happy. Problems happen in every company. The important thing is to fix them quickly and communicate clearly.

Section 7
Writing Task

Write an email apologising for a mistake at work. Include: what happened, why it happened, how you are fixing it, and what you will do to prevent it in future. (6–8 sentences)
