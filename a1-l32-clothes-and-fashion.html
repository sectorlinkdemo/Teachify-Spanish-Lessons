Clothes and Fashion

Shopping & Style
⏱ 60 min 📚 10 vocab words 🎯 Shopping & Style
Section 1
Vocabulary
shirt
a piece of clothing with buttons, worn on the top half
"He wears a white shirt to work."
trousers
a piece of clothing that covers both legs
"I need new trousers for the meeting."
dress
a one-piece item of clothing for women
"She is wearing a blue dress today."
jacket
a short coat worn over other clothes
"Take a jacket — it is cold outside."
shoes
coverings for the feet, usually made of leather
"These shoes are very comfortable."
size
how big or small something is
"What size do you wear?"
colour
the appearance of something — red, blue, green etc.
"I prefer dark colours for work."
price
the amount of money you pay for something
"The price of that jacket is too high."
try on
to put on clothing to see if it fits
"Can I try on these trousers?"
fashionable
following the latest style or trends
"She always wears fashionable clothes."

Section 2
Grammar Focus

What are you wearing? I am wearing a blue shirt and black trousers. Describing with colour + size: a small red jacket / big black shoes. Shopping phrases: Can I try this on? Do you have it in a larger size? How much is it?

Section 3
Reading

Ana is going shopping today. She needs new clothes for a job interview next week.

She goes into a clothes shop. A shop assistant comes to help her. 'Good morning. Can I help you?' he says.

'Yes, please. I am looking for a smart jacket,' says Ana. 'I have an interview.'

'Of course! What size are you?' asks the assistant.

'I am a medium,' says Ana.

The assistant shows her three jackets — a black one, a grey one, and a dark blue one. Ana tries on the black jacket. It fits perfectly.

'How much is it?' she asks.

'It is sixty-five euros,' says the assistant.

Ana buys the jacket. She is ready for her interview!
