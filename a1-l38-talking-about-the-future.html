Talking About the Future

Plans & Ambitions
⏱ 60 min 📚 10 vocab words 🎯 Plans & Ambitions
Section 1
Vocabulary
plan
something you intend to do in the future
"I have a plan to visit the UK next year."
hope
to want something to happen in the future
"I hope to improve my English this year."
ambition
a strong desire to achieve something
"My ambition is to become a manager."
goal
something specific you want to achieve
"My goal is to pass my English exam in June."
next year
the year after this one
"Next year, I want to go to a conference in London."
soon
in a short time from now
"I will finish this project very soon."
eventually
at some point in the future, after a long time
"Eventually, I want to start my own company."
promotion
when you get a more important job in the same company
"I am hoping for a promotion next year."
retire
to stop working permanently, usually at a certain age
"He wants to retire at sixty."
dream
something you really want but that may be difficult to achieve
"My dream is to live and work abroad."

Section 2
Grammar Focus

Going to for plans: I am going to study for my exam next month. Will for predictions and hopes: I think I will get a promotion soon. I hope I will travel more next year. Want to / would like to for ambitions: I want to improve my English. I would like to work in a different country.

Section 3
Reading

Sofia is twenty-eight years old and works in marketing in Valencia. She is thinking about her future.

This year, Sofia is going to take an English course to improve her skills. She has a big ambition — she wants to work in London for an international company.

Next year, she is going to apply for jobs in the UK. She thinks she will need a good level of English for interviews. That is why she is studying now.

In five years, Sofia hopes to be a marketing manager. Eventually, she would like to start her own agency.

Sofia knows that her goals will take time and work. But she is motivated and she believes that learning English is the most important first step.
