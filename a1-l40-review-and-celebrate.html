Section 1
Vocabulary
progress
improvement or forward movement towards a goal
"My English has made great progress this year."
achievement
something you have successfully completed
"Finishing A1 is a real achievement!"
improve
to get better at something over time
"I have improved my English vocabulary a lot."
confident
feeling sure about yourself
"I feel more confident speaking English now."
mistake
something done incorrectly — and a chance to learn
"Do not worry about mistakes — they help you learn."
practise
to do something repeatedly to improve
"I practise English for thirty minutes every day."
remember
to keep something in your memory
"I try to remember five new words every week."
review
to look at something again to check learning
"It is important to review what you have studied."
fluent
able to speak a language well and naturally
"I want to be fluent in English in two years."
goal
something you want to achieve
"My goal is to have a meeting in English next month."

Section 2
Grammar Focus

Present perfect for achievements: I have learned / I have improved / I have studied. I have completed 40 lessons! Comparison: My English is much better than before. I can now do things I could not do before. Encouragement: Keep going! You are making great progress. Well done!

Section 3
Reading

Congratulations! You have completed Level A1.

Think about what you can do now that you could not do at the start:

- You can introduce yourself and describe your job.

- You can talk about your daily routine and workplace.

- You can order food, ask for directions, and go shopping.

- You can describe people, weather, and places.

- You can talk about your future plans and ambitions.

- You can handle simple problems at work.

You have learned over 400 vocabulary words and practised many grammar structures. That is a fantastic achievement!

Level A2 is the next step. You will build on everything you have learned. The grammar becomes more complex and the topics become more interesting.

Remember: the best way to improve is to practise every day. Read in English, listen to English, speak in English. Every minute counts.

Well done. Keep going!
